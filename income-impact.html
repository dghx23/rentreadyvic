What this chart does — and does not — include

1
Starts with the selected rate

The calculation begins from the payment amount passed from RentReady for your selected circumstance.

2
Applies the personal employment-income rule

Where a free area and taper are configured, RentReady applies those rules to assessable work income.

3
Allows for the work-income concession supplied by the main tool

Working Credit, Income Bank or Work Bonus can change how much of your employment income is assessed.

4
Does not replace other means tests

Partner income, parental income, assets, deeming, care rules, study rules, farm-business tests and residence requirements can change the actual entitlement.

Other circumstances to check

Use the actual-payment override when needed. If Services Australia has already assessed a partner-income test, parental means test, assets test, deeming, or another circumstance that this chart does not capture, enter the amount you actually receive in the main RentReady calculator.
