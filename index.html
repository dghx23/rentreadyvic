For Victorians on income support

Can this rental work for me?

RentReady helps you connect your income-support payment, work income and a real rental property so you can understand whether the rent may be within reach and what you can do next.

Start my assessment →About RentReady
The objective

Help people on income support understand rental access, improve their financial position and know their rights.

The tool brings together the numbers that matter for a rental decision. It compares your projected income with the property you want, models how employment income can change your income-support payment, identifies relevant assistance, and keeps Victorian rental-application rights visible throughout the process.

Assess rental access

See how a specific weekly rent and bond compare with your projected household income.

Understand your payment

See how work income can change your selected payment using the connected Australian social-security data.

Improve the numbers

Test different work-income and rent scenarios to identify a more workable range.

Know your rights

Understand application evidence limits, prohibited questions and possible discrimination red flags.

How it works

Four clear stages

You enter information once. RentReady reuses it across the assessment instead of making you start again for each calculation.

1Income

Select your payment and add work or other household income.

2Property

Add the rental you are considering: rent, bond, bedrooms and address.

3Your result

See the rent share, affordability gap, Rent Assistance estimate and optional RentAssist checks.

4Improve & prepare

Test better rent or income scenarios, then review the application and your rights.

What you need

Start with what you already know

Your income-support payment

Choose the payment and circumstance that best match you. You can use your actual payment amount if it differs from the published rate.

Your work or household income

Add current earnings and other household income that should be included in the rental picture.

A rental property

Use a listing you are considering or enter the weekly rent and bond manually.

What you get

A clearer answer and practical next steps

RentReady does not just return a pass or fail. It shows what is driving the result and which parts may be changeable.
