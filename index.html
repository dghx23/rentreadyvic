What the tool actually does

It works forward from the money you have.

Instead of asking you to choose a property first and discover later that the numbers do not work, RentReady builds your rental range from your income and eligibility position.

01

Income + Rent Assistance

Select the Centrelink payment and circumstance, see the current rate in its own box, switch weekly / fortnightly / monthly / yearly, and add work and household income.

02

Property + shortlist

Paste the rental listing or enter it manually. Weekly rent, bond and bedroom count then flow into the support and affordability calculations.

03

RentAssist Bond Loan

Check the selected property against Housing Victoria eligibility settings, including income, assets, residency, property ownership, rent share and bond amount.

04

Affordability + shortfalls

Compare the property with projected income and the RentAssist rent-share threshold, then show the shortfall in dollars rather than only a pass/fail result.

05

Optimisation sliders

Move work income and target rent to see payment changes, Rent Assistance and both affordability thresholds move together.

Application rights

Then it explains what the agent can and cannot ask you for.

RentReady turns the Victorian application rules into a practical checklist once you have worked out whether the property is financially viable.

What may be requested

  • Personal and employment details from the prescribed application.
  • Limited financial evidence to show capacity to pay.
  • Limited identity documents.
  • Rental or residential history.
  • Personal-referee contact details if requested.

What is off limits

  • Questions about previous rental disputes or legal action.
  • Bond-history questions.
  • Daily bank transaction details.
  • Extra questions outside the prescribed application form.
  • Protected-attribute questions without the required written reason.
