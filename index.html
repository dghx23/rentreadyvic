Read about the gap RentReady fills →
01

Build the income position

Choose the income-support payment and circumstance, see the current rate, add employment income and show how work changes the payment.

Services Australia
Inputs
  • Payment + circumstance
  • Work and household income
  • Working Credit / Income Bank / Work Bonus
  • Future Rent Assistance assumption
Outputs

Projected payment, combined income, work-income reduction, break-even chart and flags for partner, parental, asset, deeming or other tests not captured by a simple earnings slider.

02

Assess the actual property immediately

Paste the advertisement or enter the property manually. The workspace calculates the rent share and bond position before the property is even saved.

Rental market
Property information
  • Weekly rent
  • Bond
  • Bedrooms
  • Address + shortlist
Immediate indication

Rent as a percentage of projected household income, estimated Rent Assistance, general affordability position and bond amount against the RentAssist bedroom cap.

03

Run the RentAssist Bond Loan checks

Keep the Victorian assistance scheme separate from the general affordability benchmark.

Housing Victoria
Eligibility dimensions
  • Household income
  • Assets
  • Residency
  • Property ownership
  • Rent-share rule
  • Bond amount
Why it matters

A property can be financially tight even if the RentAssist rent-share test passes, or fail RentAssist even when a renter can otherwise budget for it. RentReady shows both.

04

Explain the affordability shortfall

Do more than show green or red. Show exactly how far the current property sits from each threshold.

Decision support
General planning view

Shows the selected rent against a configurable household-income benchmark and expresses the gap in dollars per week.

RentAssist view

Shows the separate RentAssist rent-share ceiling, bond cap and any shortfall against the selected property.

05

Optimise the situation

Turn the result into options rather than leaving the renter with a dead end.

Scenario modelling
Move the variables
  • Target weekly rent
  • Employment income
  • Payment reduction
  • Rent Assistance
Find the workable zone

The sliders show how a cheaper property, additional work income or a different shortlist option changes both the general affordability and RentAssist position.

A

Application & Rights Review

This is deliberately separate from the financial sequence but linked to everything already entered.

Victoria
What it reviews
  • Permitted financial and identity evidence
  • Prescribed rental-application requirements
  • Extra or prohibited questions
  • Protected-characteristic context
  • Potential discrimination red flags
Why link it to the numbers?

The same income and property information can reveal application weaknesses, evidence options and strategies. The review can flag context without pretending to make a legal finding.

Services Australia

Administers Centrelink payments, Rent Assistance and individual entitlement decisions.

Department of Social Services

Maintains the Social Security Guide and policy framework behind many payment rules.

Housing Victoria

Runs RentAssist Bond Loans for eligible Victorian private renters.

Consumer Affairs Victoria

Explains Victorian renting rules and the prescribed application process.

VEOHRC

Explains equal-opportunity and discrimination protections in accommodation.

VCAT

Formal tribunal pathway for many residential tenancy disputes.
