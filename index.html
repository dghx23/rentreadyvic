The same income and property information can reveal application weaknesses, evidence options and strategies. The review can flag context without pretending to make a legal finding.

R

Which organisation and rule sits behind each part?

RentReady connects these systems, but it keeps their rules separate so one test is not mistaken for another.

Rule map
Income-support paymentFederal income support
Services Australia + DSS

Current payment rate, circumstance, work-income test and relevant concessions such as Working Credit, Income Bank or Work Bonus. Services Australia administers the payment; DSS provides the wider social-security policy framework.

Rent AssistanceFederal rent supplement
Services Australia

Rent Assistance depends on rent paid and household situation. RentReady estimates the property-specific amount once weekly rent is known.

RentAssist Bond LoanVictorian bond assistance
Housing Victoria / Homes Victoria

Separate eligibility rules cover household income and assets, residency, property ownership, the rent-share test and the bond amount. The rent-share rule is not the same as RentReady's general affordability benchmark.

Rental applicationVictorian rental law
Consumer Affairs Victoria

From 31 March 2026 rental providers and agents must use the prescribed application form. RentReady explains permitted evidence, information limits and questions that should not be added outside that process.

DiscriminationEqual opportunity
VEOHRC

Accommodation providers must not treat applicants unfavourably because of protected characteristics. RentReady can flag context and red flags, but it does not make a legal finding.

Tenancy disputesFormal resolution
VCAT

VCAT is the formal tribunal pathway for many Victorian residential-tenancy disputes. RentReady helps organise the relevant information but does not replace the tribunal process.

Services Australia

Administers Centrelink payments, Rent Assistance and individual entitlement decisions.

Department of Social Services

Maintains the Social Security Guide and policy framework behind many payment rules.

Housing Victoria

Runs RentAssist Bond Loans for eligible Victorian private renters.

Consumer Affairs Victoria

Explains Victorian renting rules and the prescribed application process.

VEOHRC

Explains equal-opportunity and discrimination protections in accommodation.

VCAT

Formal tribunal pathway for many residential tenancy disputes.
