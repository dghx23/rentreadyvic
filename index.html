      What a reference is (legally) and how it works
      Under the Victorian Residential Tenancies Act 1997 (as amended) and the prescribed Form 3A, a rental provider may ask for a maximum of two general references. They are not "employment references" and they are not automatically verified. A referee can be anyone who can speak to your reliability — a former landlord, housemate, employer, colleague, community contact, or support worker. The Act does not require you to provide a rental address reference, and it does not require references to be contacted automatically. If an agent asks for more than two references, or asks for a reference type that isn't on Form 3A, you can decline.
      


      Your rights around references: an agent can't refuse your application because you can't provide a specific kind of referee, and can't use a refusal to provide a reference as a proxy for discrimination on a protected attribute. If you believe that has happened, you can complain to VEOHRC (1300 292 153) or apply to VCAT for compensation under s 210AA.

        Backend hook — what would need wiring up for real email
        This flow uses mailto: for outbound and manual paste for inbound, which works today with no server. To make it fully automatic (send from RentReady, receive replies, auto-parse), you'd connect:
        
    
          
  • An email service (Postmark, SendGrid, Resend) triggered from a serverless function.
    • 
          
  • A dedicated inbound address (e.g. ref-{token}@refs.rentready.vic) whose webhook posts the reply body back to your server.
    • 
          
  • A parser that matches the reply to the reference token and writes the answers to your database.
    • 
          
  • PDF generation server-side (Puppeteer or PDFKit) with a cryptographic signature.
    • 
        

        The UI here is already designed around that flow, so the swap is contained to the marked functions.

      Income test
      JobSeeker free area is $150/fortnight. Between $150 and $256, JobSeeker tapers at 50c/$1 and Rent Assistance also tapers at 50c/$1 — total income is flat. Above $256, JobSeeker tapers at 60c/$1 and Rent Assistance continues at 50c/$1 — total income falls until the second break-even.

      1. Your gross weekly income must be at or below the limit for your household.
      2. Weekly rent must not exceed 55% of your gross weekly income.
      3. Household assets must be $27,644 or less.

      Market practice — the 30% benchmark
      Most Melbourne agents look for rent to be no more than 30% of your net (after-tax) weekly income. Some accept up to 40% with a strong record or guarantor. This threshold is adjustable.
