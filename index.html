For Victorians on income support

Can this rental work for me?

RentReady helps you work out whether a rental may be within reach based on your income-support payment, work income, Rent Assistance and the property you are considering.

Start my assessment → How it works
The objective

Help people on income support understand whether they can realistically access a rental property.

RentReady connects your payment, employment income and a real rental property so you can see the gap between where you are now and what the property requires. It also helps you understand how lawful changes in work income and available support may affect that position, while keeping your Victorian rental-application rights visible.

Assess rental access

Compare your projected household income with the weekly rent and bond of the property you want.

Understand your payment

See how work income may affect your selected income-support payment, including relevant credits, banks or bonuses where supported.

Improve the position

Test different rent and work-income scenarios to see what could bring the property closer to a workable range.

Know your rights

Understand what rental providers and agents can ask for, evidence limits and potential discrimination red flags.
