About RentReady VIC

Rental planning built around the situation of people on income support.

RentReady is designed to answer one practical question: if you receive income support and are considering a rental property, what do the numbers look like, what support may be relevant, what can improve the position, and what rights apply when you apply?

The purpose

What RentReady is trying to solve

Rental decisions often require information from several systems at once.

Income-support payments

Your payment can change as employment income changes, so the amount available for rent is not always fixed.

A specific rental

A property decision depends on the actual weekly rent, bond and household situation—not just a generic affordability percentage.

Application rules

Rental applications involve evidence, personal information and legal limits that are separate from the financial calculation.

What it uses

Different rules stay separate

RentReady connects information without pretending that every test is the same.

Australian social-security data

Payment rates, income-test rules, Rent Assistance and work-income concessions are only modelled from the connected Australian social-security dataset. If a required rule is not supplied, RentReady does not substitute a hard-coded social-security rule.

Victorian RentAssist rules

RentAssist Bond Loan checks are shown separately because Housing Victoria applies its own household, asset, residency, property-ownership, rent-share and bond rules.

Victorian rental rights

Application information and rights are kept separate from affordability and are linked to Consumer Affairs Victoria and other relevant Victorian sources.

What RentReady does not decide

RentReady does not make a Centrelink entitlement decision, Housing Victoria eligibility decision, legal finding or financial recommendation. It is a planning tool intended to make the interactions between income, a rental property and the application process easier to understand.

Where your actual payment amount, Centrelink record or an official agency decision is available, that official information takes priority over a RentReady estimate.

Australian social security

What rules does RentReady actually model?

RentReady only uses payment rules that are supplied by the connected Australian social-security dataset. That includes a payment rate, income-test settings, Rent Assistance settings and work-income concessions such as Working Credit where the dataset provides them for the selected payment.

  • RentReady does not invent a payment rate or substitute a hard-coded social-security rule when a required rule is missing.
  • A calculation can therefore be unavailable or partial when the connected source does not contain enough rule data.
  • Your actual Centrelink record, reporting history and official Services Australia decision always take priority over a planning estimate.

Why this matters: social-security rules change. Keeping them in the connected Australian dataset means the product has one controlled source rather than hidden values scattered through the website.

Victoria · RentAssist

Why is the Bond Loan check separate?

The RentAssist Bond Loan is a Victorian housing program, so its tests are separate from Centrelink payment rules and from RentReady's general affordability calculation.

  • Checks may involve household income and assets, Victorian residency, property ownership, the applicant's share of rent and the amount of bond requested.
  • RentReady presents these as a separate eligibility screen rather than treating them as social-security rules.
  • The result is a planning check only. Housing Victoria makes the actual eligibility decision.

Why this matters: passing an affordability check does not automatically mean a person qualifies for a Bond Loan, and a Bond Loan test does not determine whether a rental is affordable.

Victoria · renting

Why are rental rights kept outside the affordability score?

Rental application rights are legal and procedural rules. They should not be converted into an affordability score or mixed into a person's income-support calculation.

  • RentReady links application information and rights to Consumer Affairs Victoria and other relevant Victorian sources.
  • Financial affordability, application evidence, privacy and legal rights are shown as different parts of the rental decision.
  • RentReady does not make a legal finding and does not replace advice from an appropriate authority or adviser.

Why this matters: a property can be financially workable while an application issue still needs attention, and a person's legal rights do not depend on RentReady's affordability result.
