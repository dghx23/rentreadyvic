About RentReady VIC

A simpler way to test a rental against your income.

RentReady brings the main numbers into one place so you can compare your income with a property before you apply.

Start my assessment →Home

What you enter

  • Your payment and income
  • The rent and bond
  • Relevant household details

What you see

  • Projected household income
  • How the rent compares
  • Possible support and income targets

What stays separate

  • RentReady planning estimates
  • Government eligibility rules
  • Rental application and rights information

Important

RentReady is an independent planning tool. It does not replace Services Australia, Housing Victoria, Consumer Affairs Victoria, legal advice or an official eligibility decision. Use the linked sources to confirm important rules and amounts.
