The two prescribed forms Form 3 — Statement of Information for Rental Applicants (s 29C; Regulation 14). Must be given to you by the rental provider or agent.
Form 3A — Residential rental application (Regulation 14A/14B). The only lawful application form in Victoria from 31 March 2026.
Not collected under Form 3A Emergency contacts · Age of occupants · Pet information · Detailed financial information beyond net employment income · Bank statements · Payslips · Employment references · Rent offers · Utility connection preferences · Bond history · Legal action or dispute history.
Discrimination — what it means here Refusing to provide your own personal information is not discrimination. Discrimination is when a rental provider treats you unfavourably because of a protected attribute. If an agent asks about a protected attribute without a written reason, you can refuse. Complaints: VEOHRC 1300 292 153 · VCAT compensation under s 210AA.

      Income test
      JobSeeker free area is $150/fortnight. Between $150 and $256, JobSeeker tapers at 50c/$1 and Rent Assistance also tapers at 50c/$1 — total income is flat. Above $256, JobSeeker tapers at 60c/$1 and Rent Assistance continues at 50c/$1 — total income falls until the second break-even.

      RentAssist bond loan — three tests
      1. Your gross weekly income must be at or below the limit for your household.
      2. Weekly rent must not exceed 55% of your gross weekly income.
      3. Household assets must be $27,644 or less.

      Market practice — the 30% benchmark
      Most Melbourne agents look for rent to be no more than 30% of your net (after-tax) weekly income. Some accept up to 40% with a strong record or guarantor. This threshold is adjustable below.

      What this is
      A self-contained, offline-first application for renters in Victoria. It walks you through the prescribed forms (Form 3 and Form 3A), tests your Centrelink income, checks RentAssist bond loan eligibility, and shows how rent compares to your income.
    


    

      Not legal advice
      This tool is a guide only. It does not constitute legal, financial, or tenancy advice. Rates and thresholds change — always verify with Services Australia, Homes Victoria, and Consumer Affairs Victoria before relying on anything here.
    


    

      Privacy
      All data you enter stays in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is transmitted to any server. You can export or delete your data at any time from the Settings panel.
