What Housing Victoria currently checks

Main eligibility rules

  • Household income and assets must be within the current limits.
  • You must meet the permanent Australian resident requirement.
  • Your share of the rent must be less than 55% of the income basis used by Housing Victoria.
  • You must not own or part-own a house, flat or unit, subject to any applicable exemptions.
  • Proof of identity and residency documents are required when applying.

Bond amount

The published maximum loan varies by property size. Housing Victoria also considers the number of people living in the property, and in a shared household your share can be based on the people paying rent.

Do not pay the bond before the loan is approved unless Housing Victoria confirms an exception applies.
